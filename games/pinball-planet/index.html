    How to play

    Launch the ball with the plunger, then keep it in play with the flippers. You get 3 balls.

    • 🪐 Planet Tour A flashing arrow means a planet is waiting. Shoot it to visit! Light more planets by rolling through the U F O lanes or smashing the asteroids.
    • ☄️ Asteroid Field Knock down all three asteroids for 20 seconds where everything scores big.
    • 🕳️ Black Hole Shoot the Comet Ramp to light the lock, then sink the ball in the Black Hole. Lock two balls for multiball and jackpots!
    • 🎯 Skill shot A soft launch drops the ball into a U F O lane. Hit the flashing one for 500,000. The flippers move the flashing lane.
    • 💡 Tips Hold a flipper up to catch the ball, then let it roll and flip at the right moment. Nudge to save a ball, but too much and it's TILT!
