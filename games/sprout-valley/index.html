One quick step

Sprout Valley uses JavaScript modules, so it needs a tiny local web server.

From the repo folder, run npm run serve, then open
http://localhost:8000/games/sprout-valley/

Welcome to Sprout Valley!

Grandpa's old farm is yours now. The soil is good, the neighbors are kind, and every season brings something new.

🌱 GrowTill your field with the hoe, plant seeds, and water them every day. Rain waters them for you. Ripe crops sparkle: walk up and press Space or E to pick them.
📦 SellPut crops in the shipping bin by your house; you get paid overnight. On Saturdays the market stall in town pays more.
🐔 AnimalsFill the hay trough in the pen. Fed animals leave eggs and milk each morning. Pet them to make them happy. Cut grass with the scythe for hay.
💛 FriendsSix villagers live in town. Chat every day and bring gifts they love to grow your friendship hearts.
🎉 FestivalsEach season has a festival in town: the Egg Hunt, Lantern Night, the Harvest Fair and the Snow Festival.
🛏️ DaysHead home and sleep at the farmhouse door. The game saves every night. No rush: there's no way to lose!
