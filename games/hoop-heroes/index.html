    How to play

    Flick up toward the hoop to shoot. The direction aims; a quicker flick throws harder.

    Keyboard / gamepad: press Space or A when the marker is in the green. ← → or the stick nudge your aim.

    Swish (no rim) scores +1. Three swishes in a row sets the ball on fire: double points until you miss!

    Money racks: rainbow balls are worth double.

    Miss a lot? Turn on Easy aim. Hoop Heroes also learns how hard you like to flick.

    Pause: Esc / P / Start · Mute: M
