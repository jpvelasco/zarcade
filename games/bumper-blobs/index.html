One quick step

Bumper Blobs uses JavaScript modules, so it needs a tiny local web server.

From the repo folder, run npm run serve, then open
http://localhost:8000/games/bumper-blobs/

How to play

MoveStick, WASD, arrows, IJKL or numpad. On a phone, drag on the left side.
Dash-bumpHold your button to charge, let go to launch. A big charge sends blobs flying!
Ground-poundLet go of a charge without a direction, or press pound. Slam down and shove everyone nearby.
Dig inJust got bonked? Push the other way to slide less.
Power-upsGiant Sticky (slows who you hit) Magnet Bubble shield
BombsA red circle means something is about to land. Run!
